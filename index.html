Dil se nikle kuch naye alfaaz...

❤️ Love

Mohabbat lafzon se nahi,
Ehsaason se pehchani jaati hai.

💔 Sad

Kuch log dil me reh jaate hain,
Magar zindagi me nahi.

🥀 Bewafa

Wafa ka silsila humne nibhaya,
Unhone yaadon me chhod diya.

❝

Har alfaaz me ek kahani chhupi hoti hai,
Har khamoshi me ek sada chhupi hoti hai.

Mohabbat sirf lafzon ka naam nahi,
Kabhi aankhon me bhi dua basi hoti hai.

✍️ Zaman
